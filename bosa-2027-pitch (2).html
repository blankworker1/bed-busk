Bosa 2027
Città della Musica di Strada — City of Busking & Street Arts
Proposal for Fondazione Bosa
Draft for discussion — v0.2
Bosa has never held a comune-wide annual cultural title. This proposal is for the town's first: a year-long civic identity built around street performance, delivered at zero cost to the comune and zero risk to participating businesses — using Bed & Busk, a barter-based hospitality platform already piloting in Bosa, as the operating layer.

1.The Project

A full calendar year positioning Bosa as Sardinia's first "città della musica di strada" — touring street artists hosted throughout the town, performing at known pitches, culminating in a dedicated street arts festival. Built on infrastructure that already exists: a live pilot site, a patron directory, a pitch map, and a local guide, all specific to Bosa.

2.How It Works — The Barter Room

  • A participating B&B, guesthouse or small hotel designates exactly one room as a "barter room"
  • That room is offered through Bed & Busk in exchange for a short performance — same in-kind terms as any patron listing, no cash involved
  • Every other room continues as normal paid business — nothing about core revenue changes
  • Businesses may restrict the barter room to low season only, filling capacity that would otherwise sit empty

3.Responsible Body

Fondazione Bosa — already managing the town's cultural venues (Castello Malaspina, Museo Casa Deriu) and running a live annual programme ("Tramonti al Castello") with a track record of hosting outside cultural partners. Bed & Busk would sit alongside that programme as its street-performance and hospitality layer, with the Comune di Bosa as civic backer.

4.Economic Sustainability

  • No public subsidy required to launch — the core mechanic costs the comune nothing
  • Participating businesses bear zero incremental cost: one room, in-kind, optionally low-season only
  • Revenue upside: low-season visitors drawn specifically by the performance programme, spending on food, drink, and the business's other paid rooms
  • Digital infrastructure already built and piloted — no separate technology investment needed
Why Now

A single-season pilot is reversible and low-cost, proving or disproving the model before any larger commitment. It distinguishes Bosa among Sardinian towns already known for beauty alone, adding a living, recurring cultural draw — and, if it works, a reason for visitors to come specifically to see the buskers, not only the town.
